## Open Prism Launcher [step]

If you don't have the app, you can download it [here](https://prismlauncher.org/download)

It doesn't look as nice as curseforge, but trust me its better.

## Import the Modpack [step]

### Sign in with Microsoft [step]

The first thing you will have to do is add your Microsoft account

![first](https://cdn.r4men.com/girls-server/images/new/add_microsoft.png)

### Choose How You Want to Sign In [step]

The top option is usually the easiest

![second](https://cdn.r4men.com/girls-server/images/new/link_or_qr.png)

### Choose Your Account [step]

If you have multiple accounts, make sure you choose the right one

![third](https://cdn.r4men.com/girls-server/images/new/choose_account.png)

### Allow Signing In to Prism Launcher [step]

Click continue, and then close the webpage and go back to the Prism Launcher

![fourth](https://cdn.r4men.com/girls-server/images/new/allow_prism_launcher.png)

### Add Instance [step]

Click the <u>**Add Instance**</u> button in the top left

![fifth](https://cdn.r4men.com/girls-server/images/new/home_page.png)

### Select Import [step]

Click import in the left side column

![sixth](https://cdn.r4men.com/girls-server/images/new/add_instance.png)

### Load Modpack From Link [step]

```text
https://cdn.r4men.com/girls-server/zips/Girls'%20Server.zip
```

Paste the above link into the box, then hit ok at the bottom

![seventh](https://cdn.r4men.com/girls-server/images/new/import_zip.png)

## Modpack Loaded! [step]

You should now see the modpack profile in the top left of the launcher. Everytime you launch the pack to play on the server, it will update/add/remove mods for you to match what is on the server!

To launch, just double-click :D (or right-click for more options)

![seventh](https://cdn.r4men.com/girls-server/images/new/profile_appears.png)
